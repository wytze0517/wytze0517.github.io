DO-BSSTC
📄 Latest paper Disturbance-Observer Enhanced Backstepping Super-Twisting Control

I designed a controller that estimates unknown disturbances (like pump-flow losses) and compensates them in real-time. For a quadruple-tank system, it reduces error by 98.55% compared to standard control — while keeping the actuator signal smooth and avoiding chattering.
